Bug-001: Health-loss hazards do not apply damage or enable OK button

Test Status

* passed

Manual Regression Steps

1. Trigger a health-loss hazard that removes 5 health in a development build using React Strict Mode.
   Expected: The player image and health bar appear, and OK starts disabled.
2. Wait one second.
   Expected: Exactly 5 health is removed once, the health bar updates, and OK enables.
3. Wait another second without clicking OK.
   Expected: No second damage application occurs.
4. Click OK.
   Expected: Gameplay returns with the next player active and the next-turn modal shown first.
5. Repeat with a hazard that removes 10 health.
   Expected: Exactly 10 health is removed after one second and OK enables.
6. Repeat with health low enough to reach zero.
   Expected: Health clamps at zero, OK enables, and respawn waits until the affected player's next turn.
7. Trigger a skip-next-turn hazard.
   Expected: OK remains immediately available and the existing one-time skip flow is unchanged.

* A new `HazardPage.test.jsx` Strict Mode regression failed before the fix with health remaining at 100 and OK disabled after one second.
* The same regression passes after the fix, verifies 5 health is removed, verifies OK enables, and verifies waiting another second does not apply damage twice.
* Seventeen focused behavioral tests passed across `HazardPage.test.jsx`, `AppMiniGames.test.jsx`, `GameplayPage.test.jsx`, and `GameSetupContext.test.jsx`, covering 5/10/lethal health, skip behavior, locked display, navigation, next-turn modal state, and respawn ordering.
* Direct ESLint passed for `HazardPage.jsx` and `HazardPage.test.jsx`.
