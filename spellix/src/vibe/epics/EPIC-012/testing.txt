Feature-001: Add potion trigger UI and confirmation flow for Board and Battle potions

Test Status

* passed

Manual Regression Steps And Expected Results

1. Give one player Board, Battle, Both, and Mini potions, then open the gameplay board.
   Expected: All four appear; only Board and Both show localized Use buttons, and all cards reserve the same button space.
2. Trigger a Board potion and select No.
   Expected: The shared wooden modal shows localized name, question, description subtitle, description, and Yes/No controls; No closes it without removing the potion.
3. Trigger one of two identical Board or Both potions and select Yes.
   Expected: The modal closes, exactly the selected instance is removed, and no health, damage, movement, dice, freeze, teleport, or other effect occurs.
4. Enter Battle with Board, Battle, Both, and Mini potions.
   Expected: A centred section above the dice shows only Battle and Both potions with aligned names and Use buttons.
5. Trigger a Battle or Both potion during the player turn and confirm it.
   Expected: The same confirmation modal opens and exactly that potion is consumed without applying an effect.
6. Advance to the enemy turn.
   Expected: Battle potion Use buttons are disabled and no potion can be triggered.
7. Repeat the Board and Battle flows with a Japanese player.
   Expected: Use, question, potion name and description, subtitle, Yes, and No are Japanese and use the Japanese font class.
8. Inspect the new Board, Battle, and modal potion markup and the browser console.
   Expected: No new `ul` or `li` elements appear and no console errors occur.
